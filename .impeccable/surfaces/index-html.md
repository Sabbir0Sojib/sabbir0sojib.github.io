Scope: index.html, the whole single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the maps and research lead).

Audience and job: academics and GIS employers, equally; judge research depth and map craft in minutes, then make contact. Proof on hand: CV facts, portrait, three maps (cyclone, wind, Korail). Constraints: no phone, no referees, no CV download, no em or en dashes.

Memorable moment: hovering a map reads out real longitude and latitude in the status bar, measured from each figure's own graticule.

Unresolved: higher resolution map images and thesis figures (owner will upload), Scholar and ORCID links (none yet).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an open GIS session. Sections are layers in a Layers panel, maps are live figures you can probe. It refuses the default arrangement of a name hero over a gradient followed by three equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool neutral software chrome: panel greys, 1px hairline dividers, 6px radius, no shadows except the floating viewer. QGIS selection yellow is the only accent (active layer, selected rows, focus halo). Archivo carries everything, expanded width for display and normal width for UI. JetBrains Mono only for coordinates, resolutions and metrics. Map figures sit on light paper canvases in both themes.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is and a real map in one screen, probes it, reads the thesis as a UAV to satellite pipeline, scans publications as an attribute table, browses the map catalog, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Toolbar across the top (name, title, contact icons, theme switch). Left Layers panel with feature counts. Canvas: left column intro (portrait, name at display size, one short bio line, Email me primary, View maps secondary); right column the cyclone map in a map frame, properties strip below. Status bar at the bottom with coordinates, active layer and CRS.

FORM: GIS workspace, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 349015e2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
