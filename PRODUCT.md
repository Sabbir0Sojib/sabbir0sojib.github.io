# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step. Hosted free on GitHub Pages at https://sabbir0sojib.github.io (repository `Sabbir0Sojib/sabbir0sojib.github.io`). Jekyll runs by default on Pages; plain HTML files without front matter pass through untouched, and `_config.yml` excludes tooling files.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- Academics: professors, lab heads and researchers who meet Md Sabbir Islam through a conference, a paper or an email, and want to judge research depth and technical competence in a few minutes.
- Employers: GIS and remote sensing teams checking cartographic quality, tooling (Python, Earth Engine, deep learning) and finished project work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website for Md Sabbir Islam, an undergraduate researcher in Geography and Environment at Pabna University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. It presents his research, publications, maps and code in one place. Success means a visitor leaves knowing what he works on, trusting the quality of the work, and able to contact him.

This is a standard portfolio, not a job or admissions pitch: no "seeking positions" messaging.

## Positioning

Undergraduate work that already bridges drone and satellite scales with deep learning (UAV 5 cm to PlanetScope 3 m tree canopy mapping, SegFormer F1 = 0.944), backed by a steady public output of finished, published-quality maps of Bangladesh (cyclone exposure, wind, sea level rise, groundwater, accessibility).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a CV header, LinkedIn, GitHub, an email signature or a conference contact. Many read on phones. The maps are the main proof; they are dense cartographic figures with legends and must be viewable at full resolution.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Maps and results carry the page; decoration never competes with a figure.
2. Every claim is traceable to the CV or a public post. No invented metrics.
3. Research depth and craft visible within one screen, for both academics and employers.
4. Readable and fast on a phone over a slow connection.
5. Easy for the owner to update without tooling.
