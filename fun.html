      How to play

      Pick a level, then find five places on an unlabeled satellite map of Bangladesh. Tap the map to drop a pin, then lock in your guess. A pin inside the right area, or right on the landmark, scores 1000 points; the further away it lands, the fewer points you get.

      Easy
      The 8 divisions of Bangladesh.
      Medium
      The 64 districts.
      Hard
      The 544 upazilas.
