Zero Hour Atlas

2026

Maps early warning last-mile gaps in the Jamuna-Padma char corridor: where floods, failed roads and submerged mobile towers cut settlements off at the same time. Built for the RIMES Mapathon 2026.

Python, Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-1, QGIS

Bangladesh Prevailing Wind Direction and Speed

2026

Maps annual 10 m wind over Bangladesh from ERA5 reanalysis, with streamlines and wind roses for major cities.

Python, ERA5, Open-Meteo

Bangladesh Cyclone Tracks and Exposure Corridor

2026

Reconstructs 12 major cyclone tracks toward Bangladesh from 1970 to 2024 and turns them into a severity-weighted exposure corridor.

Python, geopandas, scipy, matplotlib

Pabna Urban Forest

2026

Code for my thesis: a canopy model trained on UAV imagery labels a city-wide satellite classifier, then crowns are converted to biomass, carbon and ward statistics for Pabna Municipality.

Python, PyTorch, Random Forest, UAV
