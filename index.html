Urban trees, from drone to satellite

My undergraduate thesis, Characterization of Urban Trees with Deep Learning Techniques in Pabna Municipality, maps every tree crown in a city by teaching a satellite model with drone imagery.

  1. 5 cm

    UAV pixel size

    Capture

    Drone flights over Pabna, processed in Agisoft Metashape and Pix4D.

  2. 0.944

    F1, SegFormer

    Delineate

    U-Net, DeepLabV3+, Mask2Former and SegFormer benchmarked for tree crown delineation. SegFormer performed best.

  3. 3 m

    PlanetScope, F1 0.82

    Scale up

    A Random Forest trained on the UAV crowns maps canopy across the whole city from PlanetScope SuperDove imagery.

  4. AGB

    Biomass and carbon

    Estimate

    Above-ground biomass and carbon stock estimated from UAV-derived crown structure.
